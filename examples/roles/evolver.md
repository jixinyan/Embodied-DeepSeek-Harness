# Built-in evolver

The canonical role lives in [ROLE.md](../../harness/agent-runtime/agents/roles/evolver/ROLE.md).
Use `builtin:evolver` in a team member binding. The Team loader binds its prompt
and tools to an independent native DSH assignment when the Planner starts recovery
after a formally failed attempt. The runtime supplies the explicit recovery brief
and ordered evidence updates. SKILL publication requires formal success of the
original recovery goal.
